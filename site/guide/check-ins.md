title: Check-ins
description: The places you stop, in Activity and on the map, with names you give them once or for good.

A check-in says you were somewhere, from about one time to about another. It isn't a note: it counts toward no streak or badge, doesn't turn up in search, and isn't on a day's page or in the email. Together they're a record of where you've been, for you to look back on and write about.

[Changelog](/guide/changelog/), the iPhone app, sends them once you turn on **Notice where I stop** in its Settings, which is off until you do and asks to use your location Always. iOS says when you stop in its own time, often after you leave. **Check in now**, on Log and Activity, adds one with a tap and needs only While Using. Another app can send them through [the API](/guide/api/check-ins/).

## Where they show

In [Activity](/activity/), each one is a row like "Checked in at Home", with when you got there and left, and how long that was. The **Check-ins** button there shows them alone. Times read as they did where you were, and a stop you haven't left yet says when it began.

On [the Map](/map/), a check-in is a ring and a note is a dot, each kind with its own numbered groups, and the **Show** menu picks notes, check-ins or both. A release narrows both. A tag or words keep notes alone, since a check-in has neither. Choose a mark or a group to list what's there.

Stops right after one another at the same place read as one, so a phone that reports a stay twice doesn't make two rows.

## One check-in

A row opens its own page: when it was, and a small map with a circle around the spot, as sure as the phone was.

- **Write about this** starts an ordinary note on that day, at that spot, with the check-in's name if you gave it one. The note has no link back: delete the check-in and the note stays.
- **Name it** offers a box and the businesses nearby to fill it in, for you to pick from. Nothing is named for you.
- **Delete** deletes it, and a merged stop goes whole.

## Naming a place

A check-in with no name shows its neighbourhood. Name it, and you choose how far that goes:

- Leave **Remember this place** off, and only this stop has the name. A one-off, like a friend's party.
- Tick it, and every stop within 100 m has the name, before and after, on the web and in any app. Use it for Home, or the office.

Use a name again at another spot and that spot joins it, so a big place or two ends of one can share a name. A name you gave one stop beats a remembered one, so a wrong match is mended by naming that stop.

Under **Check-ins** in [Settings](/settings/), rename or forget a remembered place. Forgetting one puts its stops back to the names they had. **Delete all check-ins** is there too, and leaves the places you remember.

## Who can see them

You, and Jamie as its operator, as with everything you keep here. They are a history of where you've been, kept until you delete them, and they count as part of your account.

An app whose [token](/guide/tokens/) has **Read the activity log and your check-ins** ticked can read them, spots and all. So can an agent you've connected that can read your notes. A token that only adds notes sees just what it sent. [Privacy](/privacy/) says what goes where.
