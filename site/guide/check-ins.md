title: Check-ins
description: The places you stop, in Activity and on the map, with names you give them once or for good.
---

A check-in says you were somewhere, from about one time to about another. It isn't a note: it counts toward no streak or badge, doesn't turn up in search, and isn't on a day's page or in the email. Together they're a record of where you've been, for you to look back on and write about.

[Changelog](/guide/changelog/), the iPhone app, sends them once you turn on **Notice where I stop** in its Settings, which is off until you do and asks to use your location Always. iOS says when you stop in its own time, often after you leave. **Check in now**, on Log and Activity, adds one with a tap and needs only While Using. The **Check In** control does the same from Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action Button: it opens Changelog and checks in. Another app can send them through [the API](/guide/api/check-ins/).

## Where they show

In [Activity](/activity/), each one is a row like "Checked in at Home", with when you got there and left, and how long that was. The **Check-ins** button there shows them alone. Times read as they did where you were, and a stop you haven't left yet says when it began.

On [the Map](/map/), a check-in is a ring and a note is a dot, each kind with its own numbered groups, and the **Show** menu picks notes, check-ins or both. A release narrows both, and a tag keeps the notes and check-ins that have it. Words keep notes alone, since a check-in has none. Choose a mark or a group to list what's there.

In Changelog, **Map of check-ins** in Activity has one pin for each place. Choose a pin to list your stops there.

Stops right after one another at the same place read as one, so a phone that reports a stay twice doesn't make two rows.

## One check-in

A row opens its own page: when it was, and a small map with a circle around the spot, as sure as the phone was.

- **Write about this** starts an ordinary note on that day, at that spot, with the check-in's name if you gave it one. The note has no link back: delete the check-in and the note stays.
- **Name it** offers a box and the businesses nearby to fill it in, for you to pick from. Nothing is named for you.
- **Tags** puts tags on it, like Road Trip, saved as you add or take off each one. A merged stop takes them on every part.
- **Delete** deletes it, and a merged stop goes whole.

## Naming a place

A check-in with no name shows its neighbourhood. Name it, and you choose how far that goes:

- Leave **Remember this place** off, and only this stop has the name. A one-off, like a friend's party.
- Tick it, and every stop within 100 m has the name, before and after, on the web and in any app. Use it for Home, or the office.

Use a name again at another spot and that spot joins it, so a big place or two ends of one can share a name. A name you gave one stop beats a remembered one, so a wrong match is mended by naming that stop.

Under **Check-ins** in [Settings](/settings/), rename, tag or forget a remembered place. Forgetting one puts its stops back to the names they had. **Delete all check-ins** is there too, and leaves the places you remember.

## Tagging a place

Give a remembered place tags, under **Check-ins** in [Settings](/settings/), and every new check-in there gets them as it arrives. A tag's page lists those check-ins with its notes and dates, and on the Map the tag keeps them.

Adding a tag asks once whether to tag the check-ins already there too, and says how many there are. **Tag them** does it, and **Not now** leaves them. If more stops arrived in the meantime, it asks again with the new count. Taking a tag off a place leaves the check-ins that have it alone.

## Ignoring a place

Some places you'd rather not log, like home. Tick **Ignore this location** on a remembered place, on the web or in Changelog's Settings, and from then on no stop there is kept. The check-ins already there are hidden, not deleted: they leave Activity, the Map and every app until you untick it, and then they're back. Forgetting the place brings them back too.

To get rid of them for good, use **Delete existing check-ins here** on the place. It says how many there are, hidden ones included, and asks first. The place stays remembered, still ignored if it was. Your [export](/guide/export/) keeps every check-in you haven't deleted, hidden ones too.

## Who can see them

You, and Jamie as its operator, as with everything you keep here. They are a history of where you've been, kept until you delete them, and they count as part of your account.

An app whose [token](/guide/tokens/) has **Read the activity log and your check-ins** ticked can read them, spots and all. So can an agent you've connected that can read your notes. A token that only adds notes sees just what it sent. [Privacy](/privacy/) says what goes where.
