## Everything, in one zip

In **You**, then **Settings**, under **Keep a copy**, **Export everything** puts together every note you've kept, with its day and version, every photo, recording and PDF, and your settings, in one zip. With lots of photos it can take a few minutes, and you can leave the page and come back.

When it's ready, **Download the zip**. It's there for a day; **Make a fresh one** replaces it with a new one.

## What's inside

- `release-notes.md`: every note in Markdown, day by day under each release, easy to read anywhere.
- `release-notes.json`: the same, and more, as data for another program: each note with its place and weather, your settings, your pauses, your Upcoming dates, your badges, the feedback you sent Jamie with the answers, and your activity log.
- `files/`: every photo, recording and PDF, each named for its day.
- Your profile photo, if you have one.

## The words alone

Under the export, **Markdown** and **JSON** download the same two files straight away, without the photos and recordings.

## Other ways to keep it

[Make a book](/guide/book/) of any days you pick, with their photos, as a PDF.

## Leaving

Your notes are yours: export them any time, and take them wherever you like. If you'd rather go, [deleting your account](/guide/delete/) offers the export first.
