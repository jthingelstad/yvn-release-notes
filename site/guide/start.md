## Add the sender to your contacts

Your email comes from **notes@yourversionnumber.com**. Add it to your contacts so the daily email stays out of junk. If one lands there anyway, marking it "not junk" helps the next ones.

## Reply to the first email

Your first email came as you signed up. Reply to it with anything about today: what happened, what you made, who you saw. A line is plenty. Type above the quoted email; the quoted part and your signature are left out.

The next morning's email shows your reply under **Yesterday**, so you know it arrived. [Replying](/guide/replying/) has the rest.

## Put it on your Home Screen

Release Notes works in any browser, and on a phone you can add it to your Home Screen, where it opens on Today like an app of its own.

- **iPhone and iPad:** open notes.yourversionnumber.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, open the menu, then Add to Home screen.

On an iPhone the Home Screen app keeps its own sign-in, apart from Safari. Sign in there with the six-digit code from the email, not the link, since the link opens in Safari.

## Write on the web

Signed in, **Today** has a box for a note, and buttons to add photos, voice memos and PDFs, or to record a voice memo right there. A note written on the web is the same as a reply: it files to its day, counts for your streak and shows up in the next morning's email.

## Fill in a day you missed

Any day from your birthday to yesterday can have notes. Open **Archive**, pick a day (its page has a calendar to reach any other), and write in the box there. A day you fill in later counts toward your streak, the same as one written on the day.

## Make it yours

In **You**, then **Settings**:

- **Daily email**: the time it comes, your city, and pausing the emails.
- **Writing and search**: the address you can mail a note to any day, "Add where I am", writing out voice memos, describing photos and showing badges.
- **Connected agents**: connecting Claude or ChatGPT to your notes, if you'd like.
- **Your account**: a profile photo, exporting everything, and deleting your account.
