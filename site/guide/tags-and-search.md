## Hashtags

Write a hashtag anywhere in a note, like #cabin or #maine-2016, and the note has that tag. It works the same in a reply, a note mailed to your own address and a note written on the web. Tags live in the words of the note: edit the note to change them.

A tag is letters and numbers, with hyphens to join words, and it needs at least one letter. Capitals and accents don't matter, so #Café and #cafe are one tag. A tag keeps up to 50 characters.

## The tag row

Under the box where you write a note, on Today or any other day, is a row of your tags: the ones you've pinned first, then the ones you've used most in the last 30 days. Tap one and its hashtag goes at the end of your note; tap it again and it comes out. **All tags** shows every other tag you've used.

**Pin tags** turns the row into your pins: tap a tag to pin it, and again to unpin it, up to 12 tags. Pinned tags come first wherever you write, on any device. The tag list on **Search** has **Pin tags** too.

## A page for each tag

A tag on a note, in the morning email or on a release's page opens that tag's page: every day with a note that has it, newest first.

Every tag you've used is listed on **Search** before you type anything, each with how many days have it, most used first. **Tags** on Archive goes there too.

## Search

**Search** is in the header, on every page of the app. Type a few words and it finds the notes that have all of them. Put words in quotes, like "first snow", to find them together. Capitals and accents don't matter, and a word finds longer words that hold it, so "can" finds "canoe" too.

Search looks in:

- the words of every note, its tags included;
- the names of the links in it;
- a note's own place;
- what you said in a voice memo, if **Write out what I say** is on, and what's in a photo, if **Describe my photos** is on. [Photos, voice memos and PDFs](/guide/photos-and-voice/) has both.

Results come newest first. When many days match, you see the newest 100 days, and more words narrow it.

What you search for goes to Release Notes inside the request, never in an address a server sees: the page keeps your words after the # in the address bar, a part browsers don't send. It's never logged.

## Upcoming has its own tags

A date in [Upcoming](/guide/upcoming/) can have one tag, to group it with others, but Upcoming's tags stay apart from your notes' tags, and search never looks in Upcoming. Writing a note from a tagged date starts it with that hashtag, and it joins your notes' tags once the note is saved.
