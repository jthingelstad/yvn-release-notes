## Tags

A tag groups what you keep: notes, [Upcoming](/guide/upcoming/) dates and [check-ins](/guide/check-ins/). A tag can be a word, like cabin, or have spaces and emoji, like Road Trip or 🚗 Lake. Type it with a # or without one; the # is dropped.

Under the box where you write a note, on Today or any other day, is **Tags**. Type a tag and press Enter, or Tab, and it becomes a chip; the box offers the tags you've used as you type. Each chip has its own button to take it off. Editing a note shows the same chips.

A hashtag in the words of a note, like #cabin or #maine-2016, still makes a tag, and its chip shows with the rest. It works the same in a reply, a note mailed to your own address and a note written on the web. A hashtag ends at the first space, so #Road Trip in the words is the tag road: use **Tags** for a tag with spaces. Take off a hashtag's chip and the hashtag leaves the words too.

A note's tags from **Tags** that aren't in its words show under it, like "Tags: Road Trip, lake", each a link to its page.

## How tags match

Capitals, accents, spaces and hyphens don't matter, so Road Trip, road trip and road-trip are one tag, and so are Café and cafe. Letters in any language make a tag, so 東京, שלום and Москва are tags too. An emoji counts: 🚗 Road Trip is a tag of its own, apart from Road Trip.

Each note, date or check-in keeps a tag as you wrote it there. Lists of every tag show the spelling you used last.

A tag is at most 50 characters, with no commas or line breaks, and one note, date or check-in has up to 20. **Tags** says so rather than cutting a tag short.

## The tag row

Under **Tags** is a row of your tags: the ones you've pinned first, then the ones you've used most in the last 30 days. Tap one to add it to your note; tap it again and it comes off. **All tags** shows every other tag you've used.

**Pin tags** turns the row into your pins: tap a tag to pin it, and again to unpin it, up to 12 tags. Pinned tags come first wherever you write, on any device. The tag list on **Search** has **Pin tags** too.

## A page for each tag

A tag on a note, in the morning email or on a release's page opens that tag's page: every day with a note that has it, newest first, with the Upcoming dates and check-ins that have it above.

Every tag you've used is listed on **Search** before you type anything, each with how many days, dates and check-ins have it, most used first. **Tags** on Archive goes there too.

## Search

**Search** is in the header, on every page of the app. Type a few words and it finds the notes that have all of them. Put words in quotes, like "first snow", to find them together. Capitals and accents don't matter, and a word finds longer words that hold it, so "can" finds "canoe" too.

Search looks in:

- the words of every note, its tags included;
- the names of the links in it;
- a note's own place;
- what you said in a voice memo, if **Write out what I say** is on, and what's in a photo, if **Describe my photos** is on. [Photos, voice memos and PDFs](/guide/photos-and-voice/) has both.

Results come newest first. When many days match, you see the newest 100 days, and more words narrow it.

What you search for goes to Release Notes inside the request, never in an address a server sees: the page keeps your words after the # in the address bar, a part browsers don't send. It's never logged.

## Search and Upcoming

Search looks only in your notes, never in [Upcoming](/guide/upcoming/), but a tag's page shows its Upcoming dates too. Writing a note from a tagged date starts it with that date's tags.
